Layout Wizard

Easily create your grid layout and export it into Grid Calculator Publishing Edition, our InDesign plug-in.

Enter your document dimensions and desired layout values. The calculator finds the largest value that divides everything evenly — your grid unit. If a column or row height isn't a whole multiple, the margins are adjusted automatically to fit the grid perfectly.
