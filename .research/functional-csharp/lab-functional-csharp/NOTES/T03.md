## Repo form

- Two design rules added: a union whose cases are a success and a failure with a reason is `Fin<A>` or `Validation<Error, A>` with typed `Expected` records, and a union whose cases are a value and its absence is `Option<A>`; the `ApiResponse`, `RequestOutcome`, and `MaybeInt` fences stay as library demonstrations.
