Designers Bookshop presents Grid Calculator Publishing Edition, plug-in for Adobe InDesign that helps you create beautiful layouts and setup great typography. Finally, your layouts and typography are in perfect harmony.

Our company is the first in the world to ever create such an advanced tool of this kind. The plug-in is based on Abraham Georges’ intense research since 2008 (special thanks to Marcus Garde for helping out with the initial version).

If you never again want to waste hours calculating and setting up your layouts and body copy but would rather automate most of it, then this tool is exactly what you've been looking for!
