Reason: WinSoft International publishes no direct download link. The only trial path is the Brevo (sibforms.com) form at
https://winsoft-international.com/forms/trial-scribedoor/ which requires company, country, last name, first name, e-mail and a
product choice (radio TRIAL_SD_VERSION: 1 = ScribeDOOR for Illustrator, 2 = ScribeDOOR for InDesign) before the vendor e-mails a
link. The research rules forbid filling the form, so the trial is recorded as gated. Full versions are sent by the sales team
after purchase ("contact our sales team for the link to download the full version", ScribeDOOR FAQ). The Adobe Exchange
publisher page https://exchange.adobe.com/publisher/CC/0011O000020psksQAA/winsoft-international renders as an empty
single-page application without an Adobe login, and the 2014 install guide (docs/how-to-install-scribedoor.pdf) describes an
Extension Manager .zxp flow that no longer applies to 2020+ releases.
