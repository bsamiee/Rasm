## [02]-[ENTRY_SCHEMA]

Each entry seats its term as the grammatical subject: its code-span leader names the term and its body opens on the present-active verb that term governs, so no definition heads on an article.

[VOCABULARY]: `[NOT]`

[ENTRY]:
- Leader: Code-spanned term, lowercase for a common noun and verbatim for an acronym or named surface, closed by a colon.
- Definition: One sentence seating the term as subject under a present-active verb, filled toward and capped at 150 columns.
- `[NOT]`: One nested line naming the foreign or superseded sense, earned only where that sense changes what a reader edits, admits, or refuses.

[FORM_LAW]:
- Law: Definitions open on the present-active verb the term itself governs.
- Law: No clause opens on an article, at line start or after a colon, semicolon, or em dash, in either case.
- Law: One entry states one decision, and a second clause joins on a comma before `so` or `and`, never as a second sentence.
- Law: Definitions name owners by role noun or code span alone, because a path, anchor, or link couples this page to a surface it never owns.
- Law: `[NOT]` lines open on the foreign sense's own name and close on the clause earning the repo sense.
- Law: Adjacent entries take distinct verbs and distinct content words, so the census reads sibling definitions as sibling decisions.
- Law: Polysemous terms keeping two live senses refuse bare use — each qualified spelling takes an entry, and one refusal entry names the bare word.

## [03]-[ADMISSION]

Terms reused as vocabulary by two independent surfaces earn an entry, each landing in the band whose reader meets it first.

[TIE_BREAK]:
- Law: Two bands claiming one term yield to the band whose sense the other composes, and the losing band composes it silently.
- Law: `estate.md` takes every still-tied term, because structural vocabulary outranks each band that spells it.
- Law: One term holds one entry corpus-wide, and a second entry at a second band forks the sense on first edit.

[REFUSAL]:
- Law: Sense lands here and law stays at its owner, so a term whose only reader-facing content is an obligation refuses admission.
- Law: Single-site vocabulary defines itself at that site and refuses admission here.

## [04]-[GROWTH]

Bands grow by entries inside their own sections until one section's entries answer two distinct reader questions; that section then splits into siblings at the same band.

[SPLIT_LADDER]:
- Law: Entries extend an existing section first, then a new section in the band, then a new band page, each move proving the prior cannot carry it.
- Law: New band pages land their `[01]-[BANDS]` and `[03]-[ADMISSION]` rows in the same change.
- Law: Bands split on subject matter, never on entry count.
