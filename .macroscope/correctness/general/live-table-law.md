# [LIVE_TABLE_LAW]

A live table is a roster a consumer WALKS and resolves at read time, never one a consumer COPIES. A rule, agent, gate, script, or durable page enumerating rows the table owns transcribes what it must walk, and the tell is the table having grown past the enumeration — the transcribed copy reads stale the moment a row lands, and every consumer inherits the stale read.

- The demanded fix names the table and derives the hunt, roster, or dispatch from it; the transcribed enumeration deletes whole rather than gaining the missing rows, since refreshing a copy re-arms the same drift.
- This law self-applies to enforcement surfaces first: a strata roster copied into a dependency rule, a hardcoded subset of coupling classes inside a closure lens, a language-named owner roster beside a live manifest, and a fixed member list beside a registry that mints its own rows are the standing instances.
- A closed vocabulary a durable owner fixes at its own declaration is not this defect — that table IS the owner. The discriminant is whether the enumerating surface owns the roster or merely consumes it.
