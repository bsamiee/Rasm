Challenge every existing module, type, function, and step: each must justify its existence and form through a domain rule, external contract, or invariant. Constructs that serve another construct's shape, a value stored in the wrong place or form, a misplaced boundary, or a defect elsewhere are accidental complexity with an upstream cause. Duplicated derivations, definitions kept in sync by hand, conversions between representations of one fact, validation repeated after construction, and branches over states a type can exclude all point to that cause. Existing code has no authority. Constraints require evidence from their owning source.

Trace each root cause through every producer to its originating decision and forward through every consumer. Rebuild the chain directly: state each fact once at its owner, derive every other form there, and encode invariants in types that exclude invalid states. In the same change, delete every construct that served the old shape and adjust each consumer to its absence. Rebuilds span every producer, consumer, module, and package the chain reaches, whatever the task named.

Symptom fixes preserve defects and add coupling, whether they wrap, reimplement, rename, alias, or clean up the construct. Deprecations, shims, compatibility paths, partial fixes, and deferred notes keep the old shape. Preserve only behavior and facts the problem requires. Version control alone holds old code. Replace inferior approaches for demonstrable gains in correctness, simplicity, or performance, with less code, less indirection, and one owner per fact.

Rebuilds are complete when the problem's facts alone explain every part of the structure, with no reference to its history.
