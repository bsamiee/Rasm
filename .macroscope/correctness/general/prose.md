# [PROSE_LAW]

Durable markdown is agent-facing law governed by `docs/standards/style-guide.md`, `formatting.md`, and `information-structure.md` — findings cite the named defect class and the line. Prose couples to intent (charter, invariant, boundary, ruling, trap); the fence couples to shape; a sentence whose truth depends on the current fence body is drift debt, and a sentence naming the current shape walls the next rebuild inside today's design.

## [01]-[DEFECT_CLASSES]

- Regeneration test governs mirrors: a fact a fresh agent regenerates from disk and stated invariants is a mirror demanding deletion or demotion to a tooling-verified fence.
- Enumeration anchors — a member roster freezing a census, a forbidden-alternative litany, a frozen count or seal — are findings; the extension rule (how one new row, case, or member lands) is the demanded replacement. Anchor boundary reads at the owner: a registry table at its own declaration — minting its members, or the sole repo carrier of a harness-owned external key set — is the accepted form, and a derivation demand naming an elsewhere owner that does not exist is void.
- Hedges (`should`, `might`, `likely`, deferred-decision markers), report frames (session, provenance, freshness, review narration), capability gates (deferral or defense keyed on consumer count), legacy-compat framing, imported upstream posture, and version anchors — a version literal, release band, calendar-dated transition ledger, freshness deictic, prose-stated last/free ordinal in a growing code band, or a fence comment pinning provider behavior to a named release, whose version-free contract homes at the owning `.api` catalog — are findings; allocation frontiers live only in the registry owner, durable prose carrying the extension rule. Contract qualifiers (`optional`, `if present`, `where supported`, `when configured`, `only when`, `unless`) survive, and a prohibition's consequence states in present tense — `would` is the hedge wearing a rationale.
- One owner per fact: a second prose copy at any tier is a fork waiting to drift; a pointer names the owner and the one consumed symbol, nothing of its internals.
- Near-similar rules accumulated across a passage or file, and two near-similar lines or rows a diff introduces or leaves standing, are one decision spread (docgen SAME_DECISION_SPREAD) demanding the nuance-preserving restructure into one higher-resolution ruling smaller than either source — every distinct decision surviving as its own clause, never a longer merged line or mega-entry; a neighbor separating a different failure class or false-positive discriminator is distinct law, never a restatement.
- A fact lands at the lowest tier that owns it: a sentence movable one tier down without losing governing force over more than one child sits at the wrong tier, and index-tier prose carrying spec-tier mechanism (wire framing, hash algorithms, exact signatures) demotes to the owning spec rather than deletes.
- Routing is a file class, not a section: only `README.md`, `SKILL.md`, `CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md`, and `MEMORY.md` carry links, paths, or cross-references; a relative link or sibling reference in any other durable file is a coupled-link finding, and a page never names itself. Inside an allowed class a cross-reference resolves to a live repo-relative path and a real anchor, and a diff renaming or moving a page or slug repairs every referencing durable in the same change — a dangling reference to a moved or renamed target is a finding.
- A nameable surface — member, command, key, flag — is a code span in its exact verified spelling: paraphrased in prose it is a defect, and an exact spelling matching no verifying fence, catalog, or manifest is the fabricated specific — verify or delete, never soften.
- Permission verbs (`supports`, `allows`, `enables`, `provides`, `offers`) where an owning verb states law are findings, including one smuggled into the object of an owning main verb; a package is material the owner consumes, never a benefactor that supplies.
- A demanded fix answers to the same defect classes as the prose it edits: a demanded version literal, band, or pinned lockfile-backed tool specifier, a demanded roster refresh where the extension rule is the repair, a demanded external documentation pointer in place of owned law, or a demanded deletion dropping demonstrated capability a rehome preserves is the defect from the demand side — void, with the catalog-conformant repair as the demanded form; a demanded form the surface's own enforcing gate refutes is void at the same bar, and a landed deletion answers the same catalog — a diff deleting a decision, path, or precedence rule proves the surviving owner on disk, a whole-page deletion proving it per sentence with every carried decision absorbed, verified-carried at its proven owner, or migrated, since a collapse whose deleted fact survives nowhere is capability loss wearing densification.
- A passage matching the owning defect catalog's Accepted exemplar is the ruled form; a shrink or restructure demand against it re-litigates settled law.
- Deterministic voice classes the docgen prose gate's `Check` vocabulary owns — bare sentence- and entry-opening articles, puffery and copula lexeme rosters, table geometry — are gate territory, never findings; the judgment forms the gate misses ARE findings: explanatory framing however spelled (a definition, description, or capability tour where law belongs), a hidden article opener behind a leader (`[<TOKEN>]: a …`, `<term>: the …`, a table cell opening on an article), and an oversized list or numbered entry — the demanded fix re-authors from the fact as declarative owner-voice law with every decision intact, landing near the 150-column line, never a determiner edit.
- A comment carries the one in-situ constraint the code cannot show — the why, the invariant, the trap — never the what, never a duplicate of card or index content, never process or session narration; stacked short fragments merge into one dense line.

## [02]-[STRUCTURE]

- Tables enumerate, cards legislate: a table is earned only when rows share one comparison question with atomic cells; repair is surgical moves in place — hoist repeats to headers, relieve clause tails, split overloaded rows — never conversion to lists because cells run wide.
- A table cell is one atomic unit with no internal comma: a column whose cells all hold one value is tautological and extracts to a card, a column restating its header deletes, and a cell that wants a comma wants to be a card.
- Carrier follows reader job: a repeated paragraph pattern is a record set, a list item with asserted completion is a checkbox, and a command that has not run is an instruction, never a result.
- Raw prose is one physical line per paragraph; a manual newline inside a paragraph is a finding.
- Marker families are closed sets — result, change, lifecycle, state — and invented tokens, emojis, and checkmarks are findings; invocation markers (`[IMPORTANT]`/`[NEVER]`) belong only in instruction and prompt files.
- Fence intent labels (`copy-safe`, `template`, `conceptual`, `signature`, `accepted`/`rejected`, `codemap`, `seams`) bind the body and bound the demand: a body dishonoring its label retags or repairs, a `template` body carries neutral placeholder slots by definition, and validity-as-written binds `copy-safe` bodies alone — a valid-as-written demand against a `template` body is void.

## [03]-[REFUTED_CLAIMS]

Standing false-positive classes on durable prose — flagging one is the defect, and each carries the discriminator that keeps the true positive live:

- A bare wording, tone, or verbosity preference is void: the house owns the voice, so deliberate load-bearing terseness and declarative owner-subject law are the standard. A claim naming a catalog defect class — hedge, weak verb, twin truth, enumeration or version anchor, coupled link, same-decision spread — is a live finding and never this class.
- Depth at its owning tier is by design: a reference, catalog, or template page carries the mechanism its index abstracts, so a too-much-detail or hoist-to-the-root demand against one is void. The true positive runs the other direction — mechanism sitting AT the index tier demotes to the owning spec.
- A routing row restating its destination's subject is the routing contract, never duplication, because routing surfaces own cross-references as their file class. The true positives are one fact owned by two NON-routing prose sites, and two routing rows aimed at one target.
- Deterministic classes the docgen gate's Check and Repair vocabularies own — article openers, puffery and copula lexemes, heading and index numbering, table padding and alignment — belong to the gate's own fixer, never a review finding; a semantic or tier defect outside those vocabularies stays review work.
- A finding grounded in a superseded ruling is void, the prose twin of the stale-quoted-shape rule: a claim citing a deleted, retired, or re-ruled standard refutes itself, while the cited law re-verified on current disk keeps the finding live.
- macOS TCC attributes automation to the responsible process and devolves to the shared parent for CLI children, so a preflight probe reporting an identity other than the acting binary is the documented attribution, never a broken permission binding.
- Producer-written receipts under ruled custody stand: the producing lane writes its own report as its final act, the harness states the exact model identity in that lane's own prompt, and no rail verb mutates a lane report — a demand relocating custody to the keeper, dispatcher, or orchestrator re-litigates the ruling.

## [04]-[DIAGRAMS]

Mermaid fences answer the diagram template law, never completeness intuition: a strata node earns its place only when a sourcing decision names the member, same-stratum mutual reads ride prose and never edges, a seam graph resolves interior owners home-side only, and an edge-free island rides prose by construction — a finding demanding an excluded node, a same-stratum rung, or an island's edge re-litigates the template. A visual owns one question, is regenerable, and never repeats a table; node and edge labels carry a concept name and at most one charter phrase, no signatures or versions.

## [05]-[TERM_SENSE]

- Terms the corpus reuses carry one binding sense minted at `docs/glossary/`, resolved live: a spelling a glossary entry's `[NOT]` line names, and a glossary-defined term used against the sense its entry binds, are findings naming that entry.
- Concepts a durable page names with no glossary entry binding their sense, recurring across two or more surfaces, are findings routing as the entry mint at the owning band, never as a page-local definition.
- Synonym choice among words no glossary entry defines stays a wording preference `[03]-[REFUTED_CLAIMS]` already voids, so a term finding cites the entry or dies.
- Findings carry no copy of a definition or term roster; every claim resolves `docs/glossary/` on the diff, because a transcribed roster reads stale the moment an entry lands.
