# [RASM]

`Rasm` owns the RhinoCommon- and Eto-aware geometry, numeric, and interaction kernel below the C# app strata — the exact-arithmetic computational-geometry floor and the operational geometry plane above it, one body. Its robust floor is necessary but not sufficient: the operational plane composes it into the parametric forms, fabrication toolpaths, GPU residency payloads, and persistence naming every upper stratum consumes. Where no admissible package carries the robustness guarantee the kernel authors from first principles; where the ecosystem owns the concern it composes the categorical-best engine, never both.

`Rasm` is the one C# geometry owner and the one owner of host-boundary interaction machinery — the host-free peers meet it only at the content-identity and tessellation wire, while `Rasm.Rhino` and `Rasm.Grasshopper` compose the `Interaction/` plane directly with no adapter of their own. Pure-numeric floor stays host-neutral-shaped without minting a host-free assembly; `Context.Of(RhinoDoc)` and `Analyze.From(RhinoDoc)` remain the only DOCUMENT-coupled entries, and every Eto-shaped owner is a `[BoundaryAdapter]` on the interaction plane.
