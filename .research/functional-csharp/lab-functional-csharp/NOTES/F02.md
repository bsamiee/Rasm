## Corrections and findings

- CORRECTION to the plan row: `ToSentenceCase` cannot keep `ToLowerInvariant`. CA1308 (normalize strings to uppercase) rejects `string.ToLowerInvariant()` at error severity, and `ToLower()` without a culture fails the culture analyzers. `CultureInfo.CurrentCulture` is an ambient read and off the table in a purity chapter. The compiled form is `char.ToUpperInvariant(value[0]) + string.Concat(value[1..].Select(char.ToLowerInvariant))`: `char.ToLowerInvariant` is outside CA1308, and the range operator satisfies IDE0057 (`Substring(1)` fails it).
- `csharp_style_prefer_primary_constructors` is `error`, so `DateNotPastValidator(DateTime today)` and `BicExistsValidator(Seq<string> validCodes)` use primary constructors. The "inject a value" position is unchanged: the constructor parameter is the injected snapshot.
- `Seq<string>.Contains` is ambiguous with LINQ (CS0121, as F11 recorded); `Exists` with `string.Equals(a, b, StringComparison.Ordinal)` is the membership test (MA0006 rejects `==` on strings).
- `Map(StringExt.ToSentenceCase)` with a method group compiles on `Seq<A>` even though `Map` has an indexed overload; the F05 finding about `Traverse(MethodGroup)` (CS0407) does not extend to `Map`.
- The book's `RecomputeTotal` used `Sum` and `Where`; `Seq<decimal>.Sum()` is ambiguous with LINQ (F08), so the seeded `Fold` is the sum. Parentheses around `line.Product.Price * line.Quantity` satisfy the arithmetic-clarity rule.
- An interpolated string with an `int` hole (`$"{index}. {item}"`) needs `string.Create(CultureInfo.InvariantCulture, ...)` (MA0076); `$"Hello {name}"` with a string hole stays plain (MA0185).
