About
Built by a designer,
for designers.
Designers Bookshop is an independent studio making software tools that help professional designers work faster and smarter.
The story
Grid Calculator Publishing Edition (earlier called Pro Edition) started as my school graduation project at Berghs School of Communication and was launched in 2009. I wanted a way to solve the tedious, error-prone math of building professional grid systems inside InDesign. After years of refinement it became the product it is today: the fastest and smartest way to create grid-based layouts in Adobe InDesign.
For years people told me that sooner or later Adobe would implement their own way to do what my plug-in does — but they never did, and most likely never will.
Every feature exists because a real designer — myself or someone else — ran into a real problem. Nothing is added for the sake of a feature list. I am a third-party developer for Adobe.
What's next
Affinity is a product gaining a lot of attention, and designers have been asking me to bring Grid Calculator to it. At the time of writing, Affinity does not provide a way for developers to create plug-ins — but I have already figured out a very good method to get grids into Affinity, and I am developing a standalone desktop app for both Mac and very likely Windows. I am not affiliated with Affinity.
My philosophy
A good tool disappears — it should feel natural and fill a need. That's exactly what Grid Calculator PE does. It doesn't demand your attention — it amplifies your ability to do exactly what you set out to design. That is the standard every Designers Bookshop product is held to.
